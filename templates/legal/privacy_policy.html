Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026 DPDP Act, 2023 Compliant GTU-ITR IIC Cell

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Graduate School of Engineering and Technology - Gujarat Technological University R&D and IIC Portal ("Portal"). GTU Graduate School of Engineering and Technology ("we", "us", or "our") acts as the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our academic members, coordinators, researchers, and students ("Data Principals") who use this platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we secure it, and what rights you have as a Data Principal under the law.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary data to support authentication, academic scheme management, and Institution's Innovation Council event registrations:

  • For Faculty and Coordinators: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Designation, Department, Password (securely hashed), and Portal Activity Logs.
  • For Students (Activity Registration): Full Name, Enrollment Number, Email Address, Phone Number, Semester, Department, and any additional custom inputs required by event organizers.
  • Technical Data: IP Address, Session Cookie Identifiers, Browser Type, and login timestamps collected automatically for access controls and platform security.

3. Purposes of Processing Your Data

Your personal data is processed solely for specific, lawful purposes related to university activities:

  • Verifying identity and maintaining secure access controls (role-based authorization).
  • Tracking research proposals, publications, patents, and MoUs.
  • Managing registrations for workshops, hackathons, and institutional events.
  • Generating statistical compliance reports for the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC) and MHRD.
  • Protecting the security and integrity of the portal against unauthorized access or cyber threats.

4. Consent and Choice

Under the DPDP Act 2023, we process your personal data on the basis of free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous consent. By registering an account or submitting an event form, you explicitly consent to the collection and processing of your details for the specified purpose.

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent will result in the suspension of your portal account access or event registration.

5. Data Subject Rights (Data Principals)

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act 2023, you hold the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: You can view all personal details stored in your user profile at any time.
  • Right to Correction & Completion: You can edit your profile details or request corrections to registration errors.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request the deletion of your account and personal details when the purpose of collection is fulfilled or consent is withdrawn, subject to university record retention policies.
  • Right to Redressal of Grievance: You have the right to register complaints regarding your data processing with our Grievance Officer or the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Security Safeguards

We implement strict technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Passwords are always stored as cryptographically secure hashes (never in plain text).
  • All sessions use HTTP-only, SameSite=Lax, and secure cookies.
  • All communication and data transmission are secured using HTTPS.
  • Input validation and CSRF (Cross-Site Request Forgery) protection are active on all forms.
  • Access is governed strictly by Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC).
