6) Description of Event
Under the HEI Pre-Summit Engagements of AI for Atmanirbhar Bharat,
Gujarat Technological University – Institute of Technology & Research
(GTU-ITR), Mehsana Campus, organized an online workshop on “Artificial
Intelligence in Healthcare: Transforming Medical Science with Smart
Technology.” The session explored how Artificial Intelligence is
revolutionizing the healthcare sector through advanced disease diagnosis
and prediction, medical imaging and radiology, smart and virtual health
assistants, patient monitoring, and remote healthcare solutions. The
