# Project Requirements & Ownership Agreement

## 1. Project Requirements
The **GTU-ITR R&D & IIC Portal** is an integrated full-stack portal designed to streamline Research & Development tracking, Institutions' Innovation Council (IIC) activity reporting, AI-powered proposal classifications, and student event registrations.

Key deliverables include:
- Multi-role user authentication (Principal, Chairperson, coordinators, faculties, student reps).
- Principal-assigned shared activity notifications and tracking pipelines.
- Student activity forms (dynamic fields, spreadsheet statistics extraction).
- AI classification helpers for research schemes (Phi-3 integration).
- Automated reporting (PDF, Excel, PDF drafts).
