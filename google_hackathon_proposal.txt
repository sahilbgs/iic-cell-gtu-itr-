3. KEY HACKATHON PROBLEM STATEMENTS
========================================================================
Students will be tasked with building working prototypes addressing one of the following official tracks using Google Cloud AI/ML APIs:

* TRACK 1: Smart City AI-Driven Traffic Optimization
  - Problem: Metropolitan centers face heavy gridlock, causing major fuel waste.
  - Expectation: Build an AI model that consumes real-time camera/sensor feeds and dynamically adjusts green-light durations across a multi-junction grid.
  - Target Department: Computer Engineering (CE)

* TRACK 2: Predictive Edge-AI Health Diagnostic Tool
  - Problem: Rural healthcare lacks instant access to respiratory diagnostics.
  - Expectation: Develop a lightweight TensorFlow Lite mobile application that analyzes audio records of coughs and breathes to detect early-stage pulmonary anomalies.
  - Target Department: Information Technology (IT)

* TRACK 3: Smart Solar Energy Grid Allocator
  - Problem: Regional solar grids lose up to 30% of energy due to inefficient distribution during peak consumption hours.
  - Expectation: An intelligent cloud dashboard that dynamically distributes regional grid solar energy based on real-time consumption history and forecast models.
  - Target Department: Electrical Engineering (EE)
